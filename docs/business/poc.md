# CNY 19,800 Single-Decision-Node PoC

The display price is CNY 19,800. Delivery is two to three weeks for exactly one agreed decision node. `../../src/data/business.json` is authoritative.

## Service Contract

- **Owner:** Jev Case service operator.
- **Inputs:** A completed or equivalent assessment; one agreed decision node and accountable sponsor; representative, lawfully shareable evaluation inputs; written acceptance criteria and access constraints.
- **Outputs:** A non-production proof of concept for one decision node; evaluation record; risk and limitation register; handoff notes and recommendation to stop, iterate, or plan separately for production.
- **Timeline:** Two to three weeks from kickoff and receipt of agreed inputs.
- **Exclusions:** Production deployment, uptime commitment, or operation; more than one decision node; model training or bulk data preparation; approval work; unagreed integrations, licenses, or third-party fees.
- **Acceptance:** The PoC demonstrates the agreed node in the agreed non-production environment. The evaluation record marks each criterion met, not met, or not evaluated.
- **Next action:** Approve a written PoC statement of work after the assessment recommendation.

The PoC is evidence for a later decision, not evidence that a production system is approved or suitable. See the [commercial policy](commercial-policy.md) and [claim glossary](metrics-glossary.md#claim-boundaries).
