# CNY 2,999 AI Agent Decision Assessment

The display price is CNY 2,999. The fixed delivery window is five working days after kickoff and receipt of agreed inputs. `../../src/data/business.json` is authoritative.

## Service Contract

- **Owner:** Jev Case service operator.
- **Inputs:** One named decision question and business owner; current workflow and constraints; representative, lawfully shareable examples; available acceptance evidence.
- **Outputs:** Decision-node definition and fit assessment; risk, evidence, and data-gap register; recommended experiment and acceptance criteria; written recommendation to stop, refine, or proceed to a PoC.
- **Timeline:** Five working days from kickoff and receipt of agreed inputs.
- **Exclusions:** Production implementation or integration; model training or data labeling; legal, compliance, security, or procurement approval; work beyond the agreed question; outcome assurances.
- **Acceptance:** The listed outputs are delivered for the agreed question and reviewed in one handoff meeting. One round of factual corrections reported within two working days is included.
- **Next action:** Submit the Feishu assessment form for qualification and scheduling.

The assessment does not require a PoC purchase. See the [commercial policy](commercial-policy.md) and [privacy boundary](trust-rights-privacy.md#privacy-and-submissions).
