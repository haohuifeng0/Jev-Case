# Verification, Sponsorship, Rights, and Privacy

## Verification Terms

- **Verified:** Dated public evidence shows a direct Jev call plus a runnable check or public demo under the published rubric.
- **Limited:** Dated public evidence supports a direct relationship, but a runnable check or public demo is unavailable or incomplete.
- **Unverified:** Evidence is insufficient, inaccessible, contradictory, or does not establish a direct relationship.

Catalog inclusion is curation, not endorsement, certification, ranking, or a service recommendation. Status describes available evidence at a review date; it does not establish customer-specific accuracy, security, compliance, savings, or production suitability.

## Sponsorship Separation

Payment or sponsorship does not change verification status, evidence requirements, catalog order, or editorial conclusions. Sponsored content must be labeled separately and cannot purchase a verification outcome.

## Content Rights

Public excerpts, names, media, trademarks, and linked materials remain the property of their respective authors, owners, and platforms. Jev Case adds curation and commentary only within applicable permissions. A rights holder can request review of attribution or removal through the repository's published contact route without placing private data in an issue.

## Privacy and Submissions

Feishu receives form submissions. Public repositories receive none: no form submissions, lead records, customer prompts, private datasets, credentials, or customer deliverables belong in a public repository.

Submit only information needed for qualification. Do not submit secrets, regulated data, or confidential production data through the public form. Retention and deletion are handled in the private Feishu workspace under the operator's access controls and applicable agreement.

The next action for a privacy or rights question is to resolve it before submitting customer material.
