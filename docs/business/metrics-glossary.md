# Metrics and Claim Glossary

`../../src/data/business.json` is authoritative. Counts use the definitions below and are business outcomes, not implementation test results.

## Funnel Definitions

- **Qualified lead:** An organization with a named owner, one identifiable AI Agent decision question, relevant timing, access to suitable inputs, and plausible authority and budget for the assessment.
- **Assessment call:** A completed live qualification conversation covering fit, decision scope, owner, inputs, constraints, and next action. A booking or unanswered invitation does not count.
- **Paid assessment:** An assessment with written scope acceptance and cleared payment. A proposal, verbal interest, unpaid pilot, or refunded engagement does not count.
- **Paid PoC:** A single-decision-node PoC with an accepted statement of work and cleared payment. An assessment or unpaid experiment does not count.

## Claim Boundaries

- **Offer statement:** A description of scope, output, timing, price, or policy sourced from the canonical business data.
- **Evidence statement:** A dated, attributable description limited to what a public artifact or agreed customer evaluation supports.
- **Customer-specific conclusion:** A conclusion about accuracy, quality, savings, compliance, security, or production suitability. It requires agreed customer evidence and cannot be inferred from catalog inclusion or another customer's result.
- **Verification status:** `verified`, `limited`, or `unverified` under the published evidence rules. It is not a ranking or endorsement.
- **Sponsorship:** A disclosed commercial relationship kept separate from verification and editorial conclusions.

Metric owners should preserve the qualifying evidence privately, record only aggregate counts in public reporting, and never place lead identity or customer material in a public repository.
