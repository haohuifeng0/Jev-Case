# Exclusions, Changes, and Refunds

`../../src/data/business.json` is authoritative. This summary applies to both services unless a signed statement of work says otherwise.

## Common Exclusions

Production deployment and operation, additional decision nodes, custom model training, bulk data preparation, approval work, and unagreed integrations or third-party costs are outside the fixed offers. Customer-specific accuracy, savings, compliance, security, and production conclusions are not included without agreed evidence.

## Change Control

A change to scope, inputs, dependencies, or acceptance requires a written change request. Work pauses while price and timeline impact are reviewed. A change is included only after both parties accept it; otherwise the original scope remains in force.

Customer-caused delays may move the delivery window. A delay longer than ten working days requires rescheduling or a written change request.

## Refunds

Before kickoff, prepaid fees are refundable. After kickoff, completed work and reserved delivery capacity are not refundable. If the operator cancels, the undelivered portion is refunded. Mandatory law prevails where applicable.

The next action is to resolve policy questions before kickoff and record any agreed exception in the written scope.
