# Change control template

- **Owner:** The Jev Case operator logs and estimates the request; both engagement owners approve or reject it in writing.
- **Inputs:** Current scope version, requested change, reason, affected input/dependency/output/criterion, and requested date.
- **Outputs:** Impact assessment, decision, revised price or timeline when applicable, and updated scope reference.
- **Timeline:** Acknowledge within one working day; affected work pauses until a written decision is recorded.
- **Exclusions:** Verbal scope expansion, retrospective approval, hidden third-party costs, and work started before approval.
- **Acceptance:** The request has a unique synthetic reference, impact statement, explicit approve/reject/defer decision, and both owner references.
- **Next action:** Resume unchanged work, apply the approved revision, reschedule, or close the request.
