# Anonymized case-study consent template

- **Owner:** The customer rights holder grants or declines permission; the Jev Case operator maintains the approved publication boundary.
- **Inputs:** Proposed anonymized summary, evidence excerpts, redaction plan, channels, review contact role, and withdrawal terms.
- **Outputs:** A permission decision, approved text/version, prohibited details, attribution choice, and review record.
- **Timeline:** Obtain written approval before publication; allow at least five working days for review of any materially revised version.
- **Exclusions:** Customer prompts, private datasets, personal data, credentials, confidential metrics, trademarks without permission, and any unstated channel.
- **Acceptance:** Consent identifies the exact approved version, channels, anonymity level, and authorized role; silence is not consent.
- **Next action:** Publish only the approved version, revise and re-request consent, or keep the work private.
