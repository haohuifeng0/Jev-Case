# Single-decision-node PoC statement of work

- **Owner:** Jev Case service operator; the customer sponsor owns access, approvals, and acceptance decisions.
- **Inputs:** A completed or equivalent assessment, one agreed decision node, an accountable sponsor, representative lawfully shareable evaluation inputs, written acceptance criteria, and access constraints.
- **Outputs:** One non-production PoC, an evaluation record, risk and limitation register, handoff notes, and a stop/iterate/production-planning recommendation.
- **Timeline:** Two to three weeks after kickoff dependencies are confirmed.
- **Exclusions:** Production deployment, uptime commitments, ongoing operation, additional decision nodes, custom training, bulk data preparation, approvals, and unagreed integrations or third-party fees.
- **Acceptance:** Demonstrate the one agreed decision node in the agreed non-production environment and record every criterion as met, not met, or not evaluated.
- **Next action:** Both owners approve this SOW and the acceptance schedule, or use change control before work starts.

**Canonical fee:** CNY 19,800. The canonical source is `src/data/business.json`.
