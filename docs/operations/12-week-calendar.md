# 12-week operating calendar

- **Owner:** One Jev Case service operator; all outreach and commercial decisions remain human actions.
- **Inputs:** Public account research, qualification evidence, weekly capacity, private pipeline counts, delivery commitments, and canonical KPI definitions.
- **Outputs:** Weekly priorities, capacity limits, aggregate targets, stop/pivot decisions, and a week-12 commercial review.
- **Timeline:** Twelve weeks with a weekly Friday review and explicit reviews at the end of weeks 8 and 12.
- **Exclusions:** Do not use automated outreach, displace committed delivery with feature work, store real leads in the repository, or reclassify metrics retroactively.
- **Acceptance:** The operator records weekly aggregate actuals and decisions against the targets without storing identifying data in this repository.
- **Next action:** Start week 1 account research, then execute each weekly gate only when prior privacy and capacity checks pass.

## Cycle targets

- Research 100 named accounts in the private workspace by the end of week 6.
- Reach 30 qualified leads across the 12-week cycle.
- Make exactly capacity-limited, human-reviewed outbound attempts: 25 in week 7 and 25 in week 8.
- Complete six assessment calls by week 12.
- Close three paid assessments by week 12.
- Close one paid PoC by week 12.

## Weekly plan

| Week | Primary work | Target and gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Define account criteria and research first segment | 15 named accounts researched; no outreach |
| 2 | Research and scenario mapping | 20 additional named accounts; cumulative 35 |
| 3 | Research and qualification evidence | 20 additional named accounts; cumulative 55 |
| 4 | Research, disqualify weak fits, refine FAQ | 15 additional named accounts; cumulative 70 |
| 5 | Complete research and sequence personalization | 15 additional named accounts; cumulative 85 |
| 6 | Finish account list and capacity review | 15 additional named accounts; cumulative 100; approve week-7 send list manually |
| 7 | Human-sent outreach and discovery | 25 outbound attempts; log opt-outs and responses privately |
| 8 | Human-sent outreach and pivot review | 25 outbound attempts; run the zero-qualified-interview rule below |
| 9 | Discovery and paid assessment delivery | Prioritize calls and delivery; do not exceed one-person capacity |
| 10 | Assessment handoffs and PoC qualification | Review evidence before offering a PoC |
| 11 | PoC decision and delivery planning | Seek one accepted paid PoC only where criteria support it |
| 12 | Close delivery and commercial review | Review six calls, three paid assessments, one paid PoC, quality, capacity, and next-cycle decision |

## Week-8 stop-feature rule

If qualified interviews are zero at the end of week 8, stop feature development. The operator must review targeting, positioning, qualification evidence, delivery capacity, and the outreach wording before authorizing more product work or another outbound cycle.

## Week-12 review

Compare actuals with the targets, inspect reasons for disqualification and loss, review delivery quality and workload, and choose one documented action: continue unchanged, narrow the segment, revise the offer with canonical-data updates, pause outbound work, or stop the launch cycle.
