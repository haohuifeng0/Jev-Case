# Human-sent outreach sequences

- **Owner:** Jev Case service operator; every message is researched, personalized, reviewed, and sent manually.
- **Inputs:** A qualified or evidence-backed nurture account, public context, one exact scenario, relevance rationale, and a permitted contact channel.
- **Outputs:** At most three concise human-sent touches, response disposition, opt-out record, and next action.
- **Timeline:** Initial note, one follow-up after four to five working days, and a final close-out after another five to seven working days.
- **Exclusions:** Do not use bulk sending, automated outreach, scraped personal data, purchased lists, deceptive personalization, repeated contact after decline, or claims of assured outcomes.
- **Acceptance:** Each sequence names one scenario, states why it may be relevant, asks permission for a conversation, provides an easy stop, and is sent only after human review.
- **Next action:** Log only a synthetic/private-CRM reference, honor stop requests, and schedule discovery only when the recipient opts in.

Replace bracketed fields from public research. Never paste customer data into this repository.

## Scenario 1: model-routing

**Touch 1:** `[Role/team] appears to be evaluating how an Agent selects among models for [publicly observed workflow]. I run a five-working-day assessment that tests one routing decision and documents where human escalation is needed. Is that question relevant enough for a brief discovery call? If not, reply stop and I will close the thread.`

**Touch 2:** `Following up once with the decision boundary I had in mind: one routing question, representative examples, explicit evidence, and a stop/refine/PoC recommendation. No production integration is included. Would a short fit check be useful?`

**Touch 3:** `I will close this thread after this note. If model routing becomes a current decision, the fixed assessment is CNY 2,999 over five working days. No response is needed, and I will not follow up again.`

## Scenario 2: tool-skill-selection

**Touch 1:** `[Role/team] publicly described an Agent workflow with multiple tools or skills. I help test one bounded selection decision, including evidence gaps and escalation conditions, before broader implementation. May I send the 60-minute discovery outline? Reply stop to close the thread.`

**Touch 2:** `The assessment does not implement a platform; it defines the selection question, reviews representative examples, and recommends stop, refine, or a separate PoC. Is tool/skill selection an active decision this quarter?`

**Touch 3:** `Closing the loop here. If the selection decision becomes active, the assessment remains a fixed CNY 2,999 and five working days. I will not send further notes without a new request.`

## Scenario 3: risk-gates

**Touch 1:** `[Role/team] has public material about Agent actions that may need approval or escalation. I run a bounded assessment of one risk-gate decision, focusing on evidence, failure boundaries, and human review rather than compliance assurance. Is a fit conversation appropriate? Reply stop to end contact.`

**Touch 2:** `One clarification: this is not legal, compliance, or security approval. The output is a scoped risk/evidence register and experiment recommendation for one decision node. Would that boundary help your current review?`

**Touch 3:** `I am closing this thread. If a specific risk-gate question becomes reviewable with lawfully shareable examples, you can restart the conversation; otherwise there will be no further follow-up.`

## Scenario 4: output-verification

**Touch 1:** `[Role/team] publicly discusses reviewing Agent outputs before downstream use. I help define and test one output-verification decision with observable criteria and explicit not-evaluated cases. Would a 60-minute discovery call be useful? Reply stop and I will close the thread.`

**Touch 2:** `The fixed assessment produces a decision-node definition, evidence gaps, criteria, and a stop/refine/PoC recommendation. It does not assure accuracy or production suitability. Is that evidence-first scope relevant?`

**Touch 3:** `Last note from me. The assessment is CNY 2,999 over five working days; any PoC is separately scoped. I will not follow up again unless invited.`
