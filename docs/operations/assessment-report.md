# Five-day assessment report template

- **Owner:** Jev Case service operator; the customer decision owner reviews factual accuracy.
- **Inputs:** One agreed decision question, current workflow and constraints, representative lawfully shareable examples, and available acceptance evidence.
- **Outputs:** Decision-node definition, fit assessment, risk/evidence/data-gap register, recommended experiment, acceptance criteria, and stop/refine/PoC recommendation.
- **Timeline:** Five working days from confirmed kickoff inputs.
- **Exclusions:** Production implementation, custom model training, data labeling, legal or security approval, outcome assurance, and work beyond the agreed decision question.
- **Acceptance:** The listed outputs are delivered and reviewed in one handoff; one round of factual corrections reported within two working days is incorporated.
- **Next action:** Close the work, refine the decision question, or review a separate single-decision-node PoC statement of work.

**Canonical fee:** CNY 2,999. The canonical source is `src/data/business.json`.
