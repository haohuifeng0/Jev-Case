# FAQ response bank

- **Owner:** Jev Case service operator; a human selects and adapts each response to the actual question.
- **Inputs:** The prospect's question, canonical business terms, known evidence, and the current engagement boundary.
- **Outputs:** A concise scoped answer, explicit limitation, and one permission-based next action.
- **Timeline:** Respond during the active conversation; revalidate price and scope against `src/data/business.json` before sending.
- **Exclusions:** Automated replies, invented evidence, legal or security advice, outcome assurance, private-data requests, and terms outside the canonical offer.
- **Acceptance:** The response answers the question, preserves the service boundary, contains no unsupported claim, and leaves the recipient free to decline.
- **Next action:** Send after human review, request a discovery call, route a scope change, or close the conversation.

## What does the assessment cost and include?

The fixed fee is CNY 2,999 for five working days. It covers one decision question, a fit and evidence review, a risk/data-gap register, recommended experiment and criteria, and a written stop/refine/PoC recommendation. It does not include implementation.

## What does a PoC cost and include?

A separately approved single-decision-node PoC is CNY 19,800 over two to three weeks. It is non-production and includes an evaluation record, limitations, handoff notes, and a recommendation. Additional nodes and production work are excluded.

## Can you assure accuracy, savings, compliance, security, or production readiness?

No. Those conclusions require customer-specific evidence and, where applicable, the customer's qualified reviewers. The service documents evidence, limitations, and decisions within the agreed scope.

## What information should we share?

Share only the minimum lawfully shareable material needed to understand the decision. Do not send secrets, regulated data, credentials, confidential production data, or customer prompt content through the public form.

## Where does form data go?

Feishu receives assessment form submissions. Public repositories receive none of those submissions or subsequent lead and delivery records.

## Is catalog inclusion an endorsement?

No. Catalog inclusion is curation, not endorsement, certification, ranking, or a service recommendation. Verification status follows the dated public-evidence rubric.

## Can scope or timing change?

Yes, through a written change request. Affected work pauses until impact on price and timeline is accepted; unaccepted changes remain excluded.

## What happens after discovery?

The human operator recommends stop, requests missing qualification evidence, or offers the fixed assessment. A PoC is considered only after an assessment or equivalent evidence supports it.
