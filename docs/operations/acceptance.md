# Delivery acceptance template

- **Owner:** Customer decision owner records the decision; the Jev Case operator supplies evidence and clarifications.
- **Inputs:** Approved scope, output list, acceptance criteria, delivery evidence, known limitations, and change records.
- **Outputs:** Criterion-by-criterion status, factual correction list, acceptance decision, and follow-up action.
- **Timeline:** Review at handoff; factual corrections for an assessment are due within two working days, while PoC review follows its approved SOW schedule.
- **Exclusions:** New requirements, production assurance, implied approval, and criteria added after delivery without change control.
- **Acceptance:** Every agreed criterion is marked met, not met, or not evaluated with an evidence reference and owner decision.
- **Next action:** Accept, request in-scope factual correction, close with unmet criteria, or submit a written change request.
