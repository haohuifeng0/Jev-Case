# Qualification rubric

- **Owner:** Jev Case service operator; a human makes and records the qualification decision.
- **Inputs:** Public account research, a candidate decision question, owner role, timing, input availability, authority, and plausible assessment budget.
- **Outputs:** Qualified/nurture/decline status, evidence gaps, scenario fit, and a privacy-safe next action.
- **Timeline:** Complete before outreach and reconfirm during the 60-minute discovery call.
- **Exclusions:** Do not use automated scoring, infer sensitive traits, use purchased personal lists, retain private prompts or confidential datasets, or contact anyone without human review.
- **Acceptance:** Qualified means all five canonical conditions are evidenced: named owner, identifiable decision question, relevant timing, suitable inputs, and plausible authority and budget.
- **Next action:** Select one human-sent scenario sequence, request missing evidence, nurture, or decline.

## Rubric

| Condition | Evidence note | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Named decision owner | Record a role, not personal details | yes / no / unknown |
| One identifiable AI Agent decision question | State one bounded question | yes / no / unknown |
| Relevant timing | Record a business window without pressure tactics | yes / no / unknown |
| Suitable inputs | Confirm representative, lawfully shareable examples | yes / no / unknown |
| Plausible authority and budget | Confirm assessment authority and CNY 2,999 fit | yes / no / unknown |

## Outcome rules

- **Qualified:** all five conditions are `yes`.
- **Nurture:** potential fit exists, but one or more conditions are `unknown`.
- **Decline:** any condition is `no`, the request is broader than one decision node, or safe inputs cannot be provided.
