# Weekly KPI worksheet

- **Owner:** Jev Case service operator using aggregate counts from the private Feishu workspace or CRM.
- **Inputs:** Weekly account research, human-sent attempts, qualified-lead decisions, completed calls, written scope acceptance, cleared payments, and accepted PoC SOWs.
- **Outputs:** Aggregate funnel counts, conversion observations, capacity notes, and a documented weekly decision.
- **Timeline:** Update every Friday for 12 weeks; conduct formal reviews at the end of weeks 8 and 12.
- **Exclusions:** Personal data, customer names, booked-but-uncompleted calls, verbal interest counted as revenue, unpaid experiments, and reclassification to improve results.
- **Acceptance:** Every count follows the canonical metric definition and reconciles to private source records without copying those records here.
- **Next action:** Continue within capacity, correct data, adjust human activity, invoke the week-8 stop-feature rule, or make the week-12 cycle decision.

## Canonical counting rules

- **Qualified lead:** organization has a named owner role, one identifiable AI Agent decision question, relevant timing, suitable input access, and plausible authority and assessment budget.
- **Assessment call:** completed live qualification conversation covering fit, scope, owner, inputs, constraints, and next action.
- **Paid assessment:** written scope acceptance plus cleared payment; proposals, verbal interest, unpaid pilots, and refunds do not count.
- **Paid PoC:** accepted single-decision-node SOW plus cleared payment; assessments and unpaid experiments do not count.
