# 60-minute discovery template

- **Owner:** Jev Case service operator; the prospective organization names one decision owner.
- **Inputs:** Public account research, one candidate AI Agent decision question, current workflow, constraints, and lawfully shareable examples.
- **Outputs:** Fit decision, bounded decision-node statement, evidence gaps, risks, and a written next-step recommendation.
- **Timeline:** One 60-minute human-led call, followed by a short written recap within one working day.
- **Exclusions:** Solution design, production access, confidential prompts, regulated data, legal advice, security approval, and unpaid implementation.
- **Acceptance:** The decision owner confirms the recap accurately states the candidate decision, constraints, gaps, and agreed next action.
- **Next action:** Stop, request missing qualification evidence, or issue the fixed-scope assessment offer for CNY 2,999 over five working days.

## Agenda

| Minutes | Topic | Questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 | Permission and boundary | Who owns the decision? What must not be shared or recorded? |
| 5-15 | Current decision | What decision is made, by whom, from which inputs, and with what consequence? |
| 15-25 | Failure and escalation | What can go wrong? When must a human decide? |
| 25-35 | Evidence | Which representative examples can be lawfully shared? What is a useful comparison? |
| 35-45 | Fit | Is this one reviewable decision node rather than a platform or production program? |
| 45-55 | Acceptance | Which observable outcomes would support stop, refine, or proceed? |
| 55-60 | Recap | Confirm owner, evidence gaps, boundary, and next action. |
